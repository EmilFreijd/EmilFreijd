What you feed your attention often becomes the raw material for future identity.

Better output often starts long before the creating — in what you let dominate your days.

If daily life is full of fragmented inputs, it becomes hard to create anything with direction. That applies as much to ideas as to energy. So I try to think about what I take in as a kind of environment design. Not to live ascetically — but to choose things that make you more alive, more curious, and more inclined to actually build something.

I try to ask a simple question: **Does this make me bigger or smaller?**

It's not a perfect question, but it works surprisingly often as a compass.
