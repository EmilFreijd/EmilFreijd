Friction can be a signal that you're close to something that matters.

It's easy to romanticise flow and underestimate work that feels heavy. But many of the projects that mean the most carry resistance precisely because they require more of you.

Friction reveals where you're still shallow, unfinished, or dependent on external validation. If you can stay with it a little longer, you often find something deeper than momentum — a clearer relationship with why you're building in the first place.

Three things I try to practice:

1. Staying in projects a little longer than comfort suggests is reasonable.
2. Letting the first draft be messy without concluding that the idea is bad.
3. Measuring progress in honesty and repetition, not just public response.
